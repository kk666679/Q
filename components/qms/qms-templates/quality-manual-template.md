## 8. Measurement, Analysis and Improvement

### 8.1 General (Clause 8.1)
Monitoring, measurement, analysis, and improvement processes are planned and implemented to:
- Demonstrate product conformity
- Ensure QMS conformity
- Maintain QMS effectiveness

### 8.2 Monitoring and Measurement (Clause 8.2)

#### 8.2.1 Feedback
Feedback system is established per [Complaint Handling Procedure reference] as an early warning system for quality problems and for input to risk management and improvement.

#### 8.2.2 Complaint Handling
Complaints are handled per [Complaint Handling Procedure reference] including:
- Timely receipt, documentation, and review
- Investigation and reporting to regulatory authorities
- Handling of related product
- Corrective and preventive action

#### 8.2.3 Reporting to Regulatory Authorities
Adverse events are reported per regulatory requirements [Procedure reference]

#### 8.2.4 Internal Audit
Internal audits are conducted per [Internal Audit Procedure reference] to verify QMS conformity and effectiveness.

Audits are:
- Planned based on importance and previous results
- Conducted by independent personnel
- Documented
- Followed up with corrective action

#### 8.2.5 Monitoring and Measurement of Processes
QMS processes are monitored and measured per [Process Monitoring Procedure reference]

#### 8.2.6 Monitoring and Measurement of Product
Product characteristics are monitored and measured per [Inspection and Test Procedures reference]

### 8.3 Control of Nonconforming Product (Clause 8.3)

#### 8.3.1 General
Nonconforming product is controlled per [Nonconforming Product Procedure reference] to prevent unintended use or delivery.

#### 8.3.2 Actions in Response to Nonconforming Product
Actions include:
- Correction
- Authorization for use, release, or acceptance under concession
- Action to preclude original intended use
- Rework

#### 8.3.3 Rework
Rework is performed per documented procedures with verification.

#### 8.3.4 Nonconforming Product After Delivery
Actions are taken per [Field Action Procedure reference] including notification to regulatory authorities.

### 8.4 Analysis of Data (Clause 8.4)
Data is analyzed per [Data Analysis Procedure reference] to demonstrate QMS suitability and effectiveness and to evaluate improvement opportunities.

Analysis includes:
- Feedback
- Conformity to product requirements
- Process and product characteristics and trends
- Suppliers

### 8.5 Improvement (Clause 8.5)

#### 8.5.1 General
Improvement opportunities are identified through quality policy, objectives, audit results, data analysis, corrective and preventive actions, and management review.

#### 8.5.2 Corrective Action
Corrective actions are taken per [CAPA Procedure reference] to eliminate causes of nonconformities.

Process includes:
- Review of nonconformities
- Root cause determination
- Evaluation of need for action
- Implementation of action
- Review of risk management
- Verification of effectiveness
- Documentation

#### 8.5.3 Preventive Action
Preventive actions are taken per [CAPA Procedure reference] to eliminate causes of potential nonconformities.
