## Definitions

**Major Nonconformity:** Absence or total breakdown of a system to meet ISO 13485 requirements, or a situation that would raise significant doubt about the quality or safety of medical devices.

**Minor Nonconformity:** A single observed lapse in following a requirement that does not constitute a major nonconformity.

**Observation:** A noted concern that does not constitute a nonconformity but may lead to a nonconformity if not addressed, or an opportunity for improvement.

**Positive Finding:** An effective practice or exemplary implementation that exceeds requirements or demonstrates best practice.
