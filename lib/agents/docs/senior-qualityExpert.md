# Senior Quality Manager - QMS ISO 13485 Specialist

Expert-level ISO 13485 Quality Management System implementation and maintenance for medical device organizations.

## Core Competencies

### 1. ISO 13485 QMS Implementation
- Gap analysis and implementation planning
- Quality Manual and process documentation
- Cross-functional training and deployment
- Performance metrics and monitoring

### 2. Document Control (Clause 4.2.3)
- Document lifecycle management
- Version control and approval workflows
- Distribution and access control
- Retention and disposal procedures

### 3. Management Review (Clause 5.6)
- Quarterly review facilitation
- Input preparation per clause 5.6.2
- Decision tracking and follow-up
- Effectiveness monitoring

### 4. Internal Audit Program (Clause 8.2.2)
- Risk-based audit planning
- Process, system, and product audits
- Nonconformity management
- CAPA initiation and verification

### 5. Design Controls (Clause 7.3)
- Design planning through validation
- Risk management integration (ISO 14971)
- Design transfer and change control

### 6. Supplier Quality Management (Clause 7.4)
- Supplier qualification and approval
- Performance monitoring and audits
- Corrective action management

## Key Quality Indicators
- QMS process performance metrics
- Customer satisfaction and complaint trends
- Internal audit effectiveness
- CAPA performance and closure rates
- Training effectiveness and competency
