# Supplier Qualification and Assessment Framework

## Supplier Qualification Process

### Phase 1: Initial Assessment

#### Qualification Criteria
| Category | Requirement | Weight | Assessment Method |
|----------|-------------|--------|-------------------|
| Quality System | ISO 13485 or equivalent | 30% | Certificate review |
| Technical Capability | Product specifications | 25% | Technical review |
| Regulatory Compliance | FDA/MDR compliance | 20% | Documentation review |
| Financial Stability | Business viability | 10% | Financial review |
| Delivery Performance | On-time delivery capability | 10% | Reference checks |
| Communication | Responsiveness | 5% | Initial interactions |

#### Minimum Requirements
- [ ] Valid quality system certification (ISO 13485, ISO 9001, or equivalent)
- [ ] Regulatory compliance documentation
- [ ] Technical capability for product/service
- [ ] Financial stability indicators
- [ ] Positive reference checks
- [ ] Acceptable quality agreement terms

### Phase 4: On-Site Audit (Risk-Based)

#### Audit Triggers

- New suppliers for critical components
- Suppliers without ISO 13485 certification
- History of quality issues
- Regulatory requirement

## Supplier Approval Decision Matrix

### Scoring System
| Score Range | Rating | Decision |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| 90-100 | Excellent | Approved - Preferred supplier |
| 80-89 | Good | Approved - Standard supplier |
| 70-79 | Acceptable | Approved with conditions |
| 60-69 | Marginal | Conditional approval - improvement plan required |
| <60 | Unacceptable | Not approved |

### Risk Classification
| Risk Level | Criteria | Approval Authority |
|------------|----------|-------------------|
| Critical | Safety-critical, single source, regulatory impact | VP Quality + Management |
| High | Performance-critical, limited alternatives | Quality Manager |
| Medium | Standard components, multiple sources | Quality Engineer |
| Low | Commodity items, readily available | Purchasing |

### Supplier Status Definitions
- **Active:** Fully approved, no restrictions
- **Conditional:** Approved with specific conditions or limitations
- **Suspended:** Temporarily not approved pending corrective action
- **Removed:** No longer approved

## Supplier Performance Monitoring

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

#### Quality Metrics
- **Defect Rate:** (Rejected lots / Total lots received) × 100
  - Target: <2%
  - Acceptable: 2-5%
  - Unacceptable: >5%

- **Certificate of Conformance Accuracy:** (Accurate CoCs / Total CoCs) × 100
  - Target: 100%
  - Acceptable: 98-100%
  - Unacceptable: <98%

- **Corrective Action Response Time:** Days to respond to SCAR
  - Target: <10 days
  - Acceptable: 10-15 days
  - Unacceptable: >15 days

#### Delivery Metrics
- **On-Time Delivery:** (On-time deliveries / Total deliveries) × 100
  - Target: >95%
  - Acceptable: 90-95%
  - Unacceptable: <90%

- **Lead Time Compliance:** Actual vs. quoted lead time
  - Target: ±5%
  - Acceptable: ±10%
  - Unacceptable: >±10%

#### Service Metrics
- **Responsiveness:** Response time to inquiries
  - Target: <24 hours
  - Acceptable: 24-48 hours
  - Unacceptable: >48 hours

- **Documentation Accuracy:** Complete and accurate documentation
  - Target: 100%
  - Acceptable: 95-100%
  - Unacceptable: <95%

## Supplier Re-Evaluation

### Re-Evaluation Frequency
| Risk Level | Frequency | Method |
|------------|-----------|--------|
| Critical | Quarterly | Performance review + Annual audit |
| High | Semi-annually | Performance review |
| Medium | Annually | Performance review |
| Low | Biennially | Performance review |

### Re-Evaluation Triggers
- Scheduled periodic review
- Quality issues or defects
- Delivery problems
- Change in supplier ownership or management
- Change in manufacturing location
- Change in quality system certification
- Regulatory inspection findings
- Customer complaints related to supplier

### Re-Evaluation Process
1. **Performance Data Review**
   - Compile KPI data for review period
   - Review nonconformances and CARs
   - Assess corrective action effectiveness

2. **Risk Assessment Update**
   - Review risk classification
   - Assess any changes in risk profile
   - Update risk mitigation strategies

3. **Decision**
   - [ ] Continue as approved supplier
   - [ ] Continue with conditions
   - [ ] Require improvement plan
   - [ ] Suspend pending corrective action
   - [ ] Remove from ASL

4. **Documentation**
   - Update supplier file
   - Update ASL
   - Communicate decision to supplier
   - Notify affected departments

### Quality Requirements
1. **Quality System**
   - Maintain ISO 13485 or equivalent certification
   - Notify of any changes to quality system
   - Allow customer audits with reasonable notice

2. **Product Requirements**
   - Meet all specifications and acceptance criteria
   - Provide Certificate of Conformance with each shipment
   - Maintain traceability records

3. **Change Control**
   - Notify of any product or process changes
   - Obtain approval before implementing changes
   - Provide validation data for changes

4. **Nonconforming Product**
   - Notify immediately of any nonconformances
   - Implement containment actions
   - Respond to SCARs within specified timeframe

5. **Records and Documentation**
   - Maintain quality records per retention requirements
   - Provide documentation upon request
   - Ensure document control

6. **Regulatory Compliance**
   - Comply with applicable regulations (FDA, MDR, etc.)
   - Notify of regulatory inspections or findings
   - Maintain required registrations
