# ISO 13485 Standard Operating Procedures Templates

## Document Control Procedure (4.2.3)

### Purpose
Establish controls for document approval, distribution, review, and maintenance.

### Scope
Applies to all QMS documents including Quality Manual, procedures, work instructions, forms, and external documents.

### Procedure
1. **Document Creation**
   - Use approved templates
   - Assign unique document number
   - Define document owner

2. **Review and Approval**
   - Technical review by subject matter expert
   - Quality review for compliance
   - Management approval before release

3. **Distribution**
   - Maintain distribution matrix
   - Ensure current versions available at point of use
   - Control external documents

4. **Changes**
   - Document change request form
   - Impact assessment
   - Re-approval by original approving authority
   - Update revision history

5. **Obsolete Documents**
   - Remove from point of use
   - Mark as obsolete if retained
   - Maintain one copy for reference

## Management Review Procedure (5.6)

### Purpose
Ensure QMS suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness through systematic review.

### Frequency
Quarterly or as needed

### Inputs (5.6.2)
- Audit results (internal and external)
- Customer feedback and complaints
- Process performance and product conformity
- Status of corrective and preventive actions
- Follow-up actions from previous reviews
- Changes affecting the QMS
- Recommendations for improvement

### Outputs (5.6.3)
- Decisions on QMS improvements
- Product improvements related to customer requirements
- Resource needs

### Records
- Meeting agenda and minutes
- Action items with owners and due dates
- Management decisions

## Internal Audit Procedure (8.2.2)

### Purpose
Verify QMS conformity to ISO 13485 and effectiveness of implementation.

### Planning
- Annual audit schedule based on risk
- Assign competent auditors (independent of audited area)
- Define audit scope and criteria

### Execution
1. Opening meeting
2. Document review
3. Interviews and observations
4. Evidence collection
5. Finding documentation
6. Closing meeting

### Follow-up
- Corrective action for nonconformities
- Verification of corrective action effectiveness
- Audit report distribution

### Records
- Audit plan and schedule
- Audit checklists
- Audit reports
- Corrective action records

## CAPA Procedure (8.5.2, 8.5.3)

### Purpose
Eliminate causes of nonconformities and prevent recurrence.

### Initiation Sources
- Internal audits
- Customer complaints
- Product nonconformities
- Process monitoring
- Risk management activities

### Process
1. **Problem Identification**
   - Document the issue
   - Assess significance

2. **Root Cause Analysis**
   - Use appropriate tools (5 Why, Fishbone, etc.)
   - Identify contributing factors

3. **Action Planning**
   - Define corrective/preventive actions
   - Assign responsibility and timeline
   - Consider risk management implications

4. **Implementation**
   - Execute planned actions
   - Document implementation

5. **Verification**
   - Verify effectiveness
   - Monitor for recurrence
   - Update risk management file

### Records
- CAPA request form
- Root cause analysis
- Action plan
- Verification of effectiveness

## Design Control Procedure (7.3)

### Design Planning (7.3.2)
- Design and development plan
- Stages and milestones
- Review, verification, validation activities
- Responsibilities and authorities

### Design Inputs (7.3.3)
- Functional and performance requirements
- Regulatory and standards requirements
- Risk management requirements
- Review and approval

### Design Outputs (7.3.4)
- Meet input requirements
- Provide information for purchasing, production, service
- Contain or reference acceptance criteria
- Specify characteristics essential for safe and proper use

### Design Review (7.3.5)
- Systematic review at planned stages
- Evaluate ability to meet requirements
- Identify problems and propose actions

### Design Verification (7.3.6)
- Confirm outputs meet input requirements
- Document verification results

### Design Validation (7.3.7)
- Confirm product meets user needs
- Performed on representative product
- Include clinical evaluation if required

### Design Transfer (7.3.8)
- Verify design outputs are suitable for manufacturing
- Production processes capable

### Design Changes (7.3.9)
- Identify, document, review, approve changes
- Verify and validate as appropriate
- Evaluate effect on product and QMS

## Risk Management Procedure (ISO 14971)

### Purpose
Integrate risk management throughout product lifecycle.

### Process
1. **Risk Management Planning**
   - Define scope
   - Assign responsibilities
   - Review activities

2. **Risk Analysis**
   - Identify hazards and hazardous situations
   - Estimate risks

3. **Risk Evaluation**
   - Compare against acceptability criteria
   - Decide on risk control measures

4. **Risk Control**
   - Implement control measures
   - Verify effectiveness
   - Assess residual risk

5. **Production and Post-Production**
   - Collect and review information
   - Update risk management file

### Records
- Risk management plan
- Risk management file
- Risk analysis and evaluation
- Risk control measures
- Residual risk evaluation
