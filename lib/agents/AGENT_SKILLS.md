## ISO Standards Agents

### 🔵 ISO 9001 Quality Management Specialist

**Agent ID:** `iso9001`  
**Role:** Quality Management System Expert  
**Knowledge Base:** ISO 9001:2015

#### Core Competencies
- Quality Management System implementation
- Process approach methodology
- Risk-based thinking application
- Context of organization analysis
- Leadership and commitment guidance
- Quality policy development
- Quality objectives setting and monitoring

#### Specialized Skills
1. **Clause 4 - Context of Organization**
   - Understanding organizational context
   - Identifying interested parties
   - Determining QMS scope
   - Process identification and interaction

2. **Clause 5 - Leadership**
   - Leadership and commitment demonstration
   - Customer focus integration
   - Quality policy establishment
   - Organizational roles and responsibilities

3. **Clause 6 - Planning**
   - Risk and opportunity assessment
   - Quality objectives planning
   - Change management planning

4. **Clause 7 - Support**
   - Resource determination and provision
   - Competence management
   - Awareness programs
   - Communication planning
   - Documented information control

5. **Clause 8 - Operation**
   - Operational planning and control
   - Product/service requirements determination
   - Design and development control
   - External provider control
   - Production and service provision
   - Release of products and services
   - Nonconforming output control

6. **Clause 9 - Performance Evaluation**
   - Monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation
   - Customer satisfaction evaluation
   - Internal audit program
   - Management review facilitation

7. **Clause 10 - Improvement**
   - Nonconformity and corrective action
   - Continual improvement initiatives

#### Available Tools
- `assess_qms_compliance` - Assess compliance against ISO 9001 clauses
- `generate_audit_checklist` - Generate ISO 9001 audit checklist
- `gap_analysis` - Identify gaps in QMS implementation
- `process_mapping` - Map organizational processes
- `risk_assessment` - Conduct risk-based thinking assessment
- `management_review_prep` - Prepare management review materials
- `customer_satisfaction_analysis` - Evaluate customer satisfaction data

## Agent Tools & Functions

### Common Tools (All Agents)

1. **compliance_check**
   - Input: Standard, requirements list
   - Output: Compliance status, gaps, recommendations
   - Use: Verify compliance against standards

2. **gap_analysis**
   - Input: Current state, target state
   - Output: Gap list, priority, action plan
   - Use: Identify implementation gaps

3. **document_generation**
   - Input: Template, data, requirements
   - Output: Formatted document
   - Use: Generate compliant documents

4. **risk_assessment**
   - Input: Hazards, likelihood, impact
   - Output: Risk level, controls, residual risk
   - Use: Assess and evaluate risks

5. **audit_checklist_generation**
   - Input: Standard, scope, clauses
   - Output: Audit checklist
   - Use: Prepare for audits
